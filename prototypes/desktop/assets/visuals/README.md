# Paisagem noturna do site

`night-flight.png` foi gerada com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` em 9 de outubro de 2026 e incorporada ao workspace, sem alteração da imagem original. É uma ambientação para o site; não substitui a marca Orchestrix.

## Prompt final

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide atmospheric background image for the Orchestrix website, landscape 16:9. Primary request: the feeling of a calm night flight through a valley of steep rocky mountains, seen from midair looking forward toward distant layered peaks. Elegant cinematic realism with sharply faceted slate-rock ridges, cool moonlight, thin atmospheric haze in the valley. Restrained deep navy (#0B1426) and blue graphite with subtle indigo (#6366F1) rim light. Large dark open sky in the upper half, no large moon or galaxies. Rock silhouettes emerge from the lower corners and recede toward the centre, creating depth and a sense of moving through the valley. Keep the overall scene dark and restrained so white website text remains readable. No text, logos, icons, UI, aircraft, people, buildings, illustration border, or watermark. The artwork must be landscape and photographic in texture rather than a generic vector mountain illustration.

A composição e as cores foram examinadas antes da integração. Sombras/gradientes são camadas CSS, enquanto o campo de estrelas é canvas. Não há animação nem alteração raster embutida nesse arquivo.
